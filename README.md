# The Collapse of the Semantic Middle

**Bidirectional LLM Mediation and the Risk of Communicative Atrophy**

This repository contains a conceptual paper about AI-mediated communication,
linguistic sovereignty, and the risk that large language models may increasingly
occupy the human-operated middle of communication.

The paper is a cautionary think piece, not a claim that linguistic collapse is
inevitable or already established. It proposes a risk model in which sender-side
LLM expansion and receiver-side LLM summarisation become ordinary enough that
humans remain at the edges of an exchange while machines traverse much of the
expressive and interpretive substance between them.

## Core Concepts

The paper introduces and develops the following working vocabulary:

- **Semantic middle** - the human-operated space in which compressed intention is
  expanded into communicable form and received language is reconstructed into
  situated meaning.
- **Expressive offloading** - delegating formulation, tone, structure, and
  elaboration of one's intended message to an AI system.
- **Interpretive offloading** - delegating reading, summarisation, inference,
  classification, and response selection to an AI system.
- **Communicative sovereignty** - the practical ability to formulate, transmit,
  receive, inspect, contest, and repair meaning without mandatory dependence on
  an intermediary.
- **Semantic reachability** - whether an idea can be carried from one mind to
  another with enough structure and nuance to be meaningfully received.
- **Semantic closure** - a self-sealing condition in which reduced expressive
  and receptive capacity prevents a population from adequately diagnosing or
  communicating that same loss.
